Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is the Client Scam Detector free?

Yes. It is free to use in your browser. No login, subscription, or account is required.

2. Are my client details uploaded?

No. The tool runs locally in your browser and does not upload, save, or store your selections.

3. Can a real client still show warning signs?

Yes. A legitimate client can still have unclear payment terms, vague scope, or messy communication. Use the result to decide what to clarify before you start.

4. Should freelancers require an upfront deposit?

For most freelance projects, a 25%-50% deposit is a smart safety baseline. It helps confirm the client is serious before you invest time in delivery.
