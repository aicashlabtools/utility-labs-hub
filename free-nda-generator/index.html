MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on this June 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Your Business Name ("Disclosing Party"), and Client Company Name ("Receiving Party").
1. Purpose. The parties wish to discuss a potential freelance project, including services, creative direction, pricing, timelines, and project requirements. During those discussions, either party may share business, creative, technical, or financial information that should remain confidential.
2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" includes proposals, project briefs, creative files, strategy notes, pricing, client information, business plans, source files, and any other information shared for the potential freelance project that is marked confidential or should reasonably be treated as confidential.
3. Obligations. Each receiving party shall keep Confidential Information private, use it only for evaluating or completing the project, and avoid sharing it with anyone who does not need access for the agreed purpose.
4. Governing Law. This Agreement and any disputes arising out of it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
