Frequently Asked Questions
What is freelancer capacity?
Freelancer capacity is the client-delivery time left after planned time off, administration, and your selected safety buffer are deducted from your working schedule.
How are vacation days converted into weekly capacity?
The planner estimates hours per working day, multiplies by monthly days off, and divides by 4.33 weeks. One eight-hour day off therefore reduces weekly capacity by about 1.85 hours.
Should I book every usable client hour?
The usable-client-hours result already respects the safety buffer you selected. Keep the buffer realistic for revisions, delays, illness, and unplanned work.
How does the planner decide whether a new project fits?
It divides total project hours by deadline weeks and compares that weekly load with remaining safe capacity after current commitments.
How does project roll-off affect the forecast?
A current project remains active through its stated weeks remaining. Its weekly hours become available in the following forecast week.
Are my project details uploaded?
No. The planner runs locally in your browser and does not upload or store your inputs.
