Understanding Business Runway

Your runway is the amount of time your business can operate before cash reserves run out. Tracking burn rate and revenue gaps helps you make smarter growth decisions before money becomes a problem.

Free Business Runway Calculator for Cash Burn Planning

Use this free business runway calculator to estimate how many months your cash reserves can support your business.

The calculator is useful for monthly expense planning, revenue-gap checks, cash reserve reviews, and deciding whether to cut costs or increase sales.
