      Frequently Asked Questions

      When should I ask for a testimonial?

      Ask within 24 to 48 hours of a positive delivery moment, approval message, measurable win, or repeat booking. The result is still fresh and easier for the client to describe.

      Should I provide a draft?

      Yes. A short editable draft reduces writer's block. Make sure the client can change it freely so the final testimonial still sounds like them.

      Can I ask for a referral too?

      You can, but a single ask usually converts better. Use the referral objective only when the client is enthusiastic or already mentions people who need similar help.

      Can I use their logo with the testimonial?

      Get explicit written permission before publishing a client logo, name, headshot, or company details on your website or portfolio.
