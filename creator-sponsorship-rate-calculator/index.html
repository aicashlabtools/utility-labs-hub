FAQ

How does this influencer rate calculator work?

It combines audience size, engagement, platform, production effort, deliverables, and licensing terms to estimate Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, newsletter, and podcast sponsorship pricing.

How much should creators charge for sponsorships?

Start with platform CPM, audience size, engagement, niche value, deliverables, production complexity, and commercial rights, then compare the recommended rate against brand budget and creator demand.

Should usage rights cost extra?

Yes. Organic posting is different from allowing the brand to reuse your content on its own website, social channels, emails, or ads.

What are paid advertising rights?

Paid advertising rights let a brand run your sponsored content as paid media, such as whitelisting, dark posts, or Spark Ads. That should be priced separately.

Should exclusivity increase the rate?

Yes. Exclusivity blocks you from accepting competitor deals, so it should compensate you for limited future earning potential.

Should creators request a deposit?

A 50% upfront deposit is a professional standard that protects production time and reduces non-payment risk.
