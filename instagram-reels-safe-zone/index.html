How to Use the Instagram Reels Safe Zone Checker

Instagram Reels can appear in multiple contexts, including the Reels tab and the main feed. This free Instagram Reels safe zone checker helps you review whether captions, hook text, logos, or key visuals may overlap common mobile UI areas before you post.

Why Does Reels Text Get Cut Off in Feed?

A Reel is often edited as a 9:16 vertical video, but it may be previewed or surfaced in feed contexts where cropping and interface overlays change what viewers see. Text placed too close to the top, bottom, or right side can become harder to read. Keeping important content inside the recommended safe area gives your Reels more room across mobile placements.
