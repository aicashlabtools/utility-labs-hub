Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Free Invoice Generator free?

Yes. This tool is free to use in your browser.

Do I need to sign up?

No signup is required. Open the tool, enter your details, and use the result right away.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The tool is designed to run locally in your browser whenever possible, so your inputs are not saved or sent to our servers.

Can I use this for freelance invoices, consultant invoices, agency invoices, creative services, and client billing?

Yes. It is designed for freelance invoices, consultant invoices, agency invoices, creative services, and client billing.

Can I copy, export, or save the result?

Yes. You can print or save the invoice as a PDF from your browser.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is responsive, though larger screens are usually easier for detailed editing or review.
