Frequently Asked Questions

            

                
How is productivity debt calculated?
This calculator combines interruption recovery time, weekly admin work, and a context-switching penalty, then compares the result with your weekly capacity and hourly value.

                
What counts as freelancer productivity debt?
Productivity debt is unpaid or low-leverage friction that reduces billable output, including manual admin, scattered communication, duplicated tasks, and recovery time after interruptions.

                
Can automation recover billable hours?
Yes. Invoicing systems, scheduling links, proposal templates, intake forms, and reusable follow-up workflows can recover hours that were previously spent on repeat admin.

                
How often should freelancers review productivity debt?
Review it monthly or whenever your client load changes. Admin bloat often appears gradually as more clients, channels, and recurring tasks accumulate.

                
Is my calculator data saved?
No account is required. The calculator runs in your browser and does not need to send your productivity inputs to a server.
