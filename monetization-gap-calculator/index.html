Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I getting traffic but not making money?

Traffic only turns into revenue when you have a clear path from viewer to subscriber to buyer. Low LTV, weak offers, or missing products can make a large audience underperform.

How many products should a creator sell?

Many creators do better with a simple offer ladder: a free lead magnet, a low-ticket starter product, a core paid product, and a premium option for serious buyers.
