    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is value-based pricing?

    Value-based pricing sets a project fee partly around the economic value of the outcome to the client, while still respecting your delivery cost, risk, and minimum pricing floor.

    When is value pricing better than hourly pricing?

    It is usually more suitable when the outcome has meaningful business value, the scope is clear, and you can estimate client impact with reasonable confidence.

    When should freelancers charge hourly?

    Hourly pricing is useful for discovery, open-ended work, unclear scope, frequent changes, or projects where the client-value estimate is weak.

    How do I estimate client value?

    Use credible revenue gains, cost savings, time savings, risk reduction, or strategic impact. Prefer conservative assumptions that the client can understand and validate.

    What if project scope is uncertain?

    Use paid discovery, milestones, change-order fees, or a hybrid model. A fixed fee without scope protection can turn efficiency upside into uncompensated work.

    Can I combine value pricing and hourly billing?

    Yes. A common hybrid is a fixed fee for defined outcomes plus an hourly or day rate for additions outside the agreed scope.
