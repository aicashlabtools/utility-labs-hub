Free Online Teleprompter

Use this free online teleprompter to paste a script, control reading speed, adjust font size, and record smoother videos without installing software. It is built for creators, YouTubers, educators, coaches, and freelancers who need a simple browser teleprompter for tutorials, presentations, courses, and short-form videos.

The script teleprompter keeps your text local in your browser, so you can rehearse or record without uploading private notes. Use fullscreen mode for a cleaner recording setup, mirror mode for teleprompter glass, and speed controls to match your natural speaking pace.

Long scripts are supported too. Paste your full draft, review the word count and estimated read time, then launch the teleprompter when you are ready to record.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this teleprompter free?

Yes. The teleprompter runs in your browser and does not require signup.

Can I paste a video script?

Yes. Paste your script, adjust the speed, and use it while recording videos or tutorials.

Can I use this for YouTube videos?

Yes. It works well for YouTube videos, tutorials, online courses, product demos, Reels, Shorts, and talking-head recordings.

Does the teleprompter work in fullscreen?

Yes. Use the fullscreen control inside the production preview. Your browser may ask for permission before entering fullscreen mode.

Can I change the scrolling speed?

Yes. Use the speed slider before or during recording. You can also use the Up and Down arrow keys while the teleprompter is running.

Are my scripts uploaded?

No. Your script stays in your local browser session and is not uploaded, saved, or sent to our servers.
