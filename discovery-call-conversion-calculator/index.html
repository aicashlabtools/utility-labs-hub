Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good discovery call close rate?

A useful target depends on lead quality and offer fit. Warm, well-qualified calls often close at a higher rate than cold or loosely screened calls, so compare your rate with your own funnel over time.

How is discovery call conversion rate calculated?

Divide closed clients by completed discovery calls, then multiply by 100. Track booked-to-completed rate separately so no-shows do not hide the sales conversation's actual performance.

Should no-shows count against close rate?

Track them as a separate funnel stage. This reveals whether the problem is attendance or the call itself and makes the revenue-loss estimate more useful.

Does follow-up affect the estimate?

Yes. The calculator models weak follow-up as recoverable revenue opportunity and uses follow-up attempts in its recommendations.

Are my inputs stored?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and does not upload or store your inputs.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes. The calculator stacks into a single-column layout on narrow screens.
