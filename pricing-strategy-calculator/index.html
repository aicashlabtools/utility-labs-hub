What Is A Pricing Strategy Calculator?

A pricing strategy calculator helps creators test whether a digital product can realistically hit a monthly revenue goal. Instead of guessing, you can compare traffic, conversion rate, and product price in one simple revenue planning model.

How To Estimate Digital Product Revenue

Start with the traffic you already have, choose a digital product pricing strategy, and set a realistic conversion rate. The calculator estimates monthly revenue, then shows the traffic or price needed to close the gap.

Why Conversion Rate Matters

Conversion rate changes the entire model. A product converting at 1% needs twice as much traffic as one converting at 2%, so small improvements to the offer, page, or audience fit can make the revenue target easier to reach.

Traffic vs Pricing Tradeoffs

More traffic is not always the fastest path to revenue. Sometimes a stronger offer, clearer positioning, or a slightly higher price can produce a better result than chasing a larger audience.
