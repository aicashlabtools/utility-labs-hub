How To Stop Working For Free As A Freelancer

Learn how to spot scope creep, calculate unpaid hours, and protect your freelance projects from extra work that was never priced.

What working for free usually looks like

Freelancers often lose money through small extras: another revision, another meeting, a quick admin request, or a deliverable that was not included in the original scope.

Turn vague scope creep into numbers

When you calculate unpaid hours, the problem becomes easier to explain. A few extra hours each week can become a meaningful monthly revenue leak.

How to reduce unpaid work

  1. Define deliverables clearly before starting.
  2. List revision limits in the statement of work.
  3. Track extra requests as they happen.
  4. Charge for work that falls outside scope.
