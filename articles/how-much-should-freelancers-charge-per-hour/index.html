How Much Should Freelancers Charge Per Hour?

A practical guide to setting freelance hourly rates based on income goals, taxes, expenses, time off, and billable hours.

Start with your income goal

Your freelance rate should cover more than the time spent doing client work. It also needs to support taxes, software, admin time, sales time, revisions, and time off.

Why billable hours matter

Most freelancers do not bill every working hour. Client communication, proposals, revisions, learning, and admin work reduce the number of paid hours available each week.

A simple rate-setting workflow

  1. Choose your annual income goal.
  2. Add taxes, savings, and overhead.
  3. Estimate realistic billable hours.
  4. Calculate the hourly rate needed to support the business.
