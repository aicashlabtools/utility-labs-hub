Good linkedin post work starts with accurate inputs and a clear reason for checking them. A few disciplined habits make the result easier to trust and act on.

Quick answers

Should I use estimates or final numbers?

Estimates are fine for an early comparison. Before committing to a price, plan, or published asset, replace them with the most current information available.

How many versions should I test?

Start with the expected case and one conservative alternative. Add another version only when it represents a decision you might realistically make.

What matters most in the result?

Focus on the finding that could change the decision. Minor differences are less useful than a clear issue with cost, clarity, timing, or fit.

Best-practice checklist

  • Use inputs from the same time period, currency, format, and platform context.
  • Keep one variable fixed when comparing alternatives.
  • Check the result against client terms, platform rules, or actual business costs.
  • Run a final check after the draft or numbers change.
