Free Caption Line Splitter for Short-Form Videos

Use this free caption line splitter to turn long script text into shorter lines for TikTok captions, Instagram Reels subtitles, and YouTube Shorts overlays.

The tool is useful for creators editing in CapCut, Premiere, Canva, or any workflow where caption readability matters.

Pair it with the teleprompter or script time calculator when you want to write, time, record, and format a script in one workflow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Caption Line Splitter free?

Yes. This tool is free to use in your browser.

Do I need to sign up?

No signup is required. Open the tool, enter your details, and use the result right away.

No. The tool is designed to run locally in your browser whenever possible, so your inputs are not saved or sent to our servers.

Yes. It is designed for TikTok captions, Instagram Reels subtitles, YouTube Shorts, and faceless video scripts.

Can I copy, export, or save the result?

Yes. You can copy the split caption lines into your editor.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is responsive, though larger screens are usually easier for detailed editing or review.
