Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate ROI on an AI subscription?

Add measurable monthly value from time saved, additional revenue, and costs replaced. Subtract total monthly AI cost, divide the net value by that cost, and multiply by 100.

What counts as an AI expense?

Include subscriptions, API and token usage, agent execution, automation platforms, generation credits, add-ons, and other recurring AI costs.

Should API token costs be included in AI ROI?

Yes. API and token usage are part of the monthly cost required to produce the measured value.

How do I calculate the value of time saved by AI?

Multiply hours saved per week by 4.33, then multiply by a reasonable hourly value for your work.

What is a good ROI for AI tools?

This calculator treats 300% or more as excellent, 100–299% as strong, 1–99% as positive, approximately 0% as break-even, and anything below 0% as negative.

How do I know if an AI subscription is worth keeping?

Compare its contribution with the stack’s total measurable value and check for overlapping features, low usage, or value that cannot be verified.

Should I count revenue generated by AI?

Count only additional revenue you can reasonably attribute to AI, not your entire business revenue.

How do I avoid double-counting AI benefits?

Do not count the same outcome twice. If saved time directly produced the additional revenue you entered, count both only when they represent separate measurable benefits.

Can this calculator evaluate AI agents and automations?

Yes. Add execution or platform costs under Agents & Automations, then include only the measurable value those workflows create.

Does this calculator store my financial data?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and does not upload or store your inputs.
