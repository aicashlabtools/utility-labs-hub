Frequently Asked Questions

How much should freelancers charge for monthly retainers?

Pricing should reflect value delivery and execution overhead, matching your underlying hourly floor plus a premium for guaranteed calendar accessibility and priority fulfillment speeds.

How do I price a retainer package?

Anchor the structure on access tier parameters (Basic, Growth, Advisor). Calculate the explicit asset quantities or sprint hours required, then adjust margins against client sizing constraints.

How many retainer clients should I have?

Most independent premium operators focus on 3 to 6 concurrent retainer partnerships to maximize contract value outputs without triggering cognitive burnout patterns.

Should I charge upfront for retainers?

Yes. Recurring retainer accounts must always be paid upfront on the 1st of each service period before any task assignments, sprint tasks, or consulting schedules begin.

How do I avoid scope creep in a retainer agreement?

Explicitly track work logs and define out-of-scope tasks line-by-line. Include contract clauses converting surplus work items automatically into standard hourly billing additions.
