            Placement SEO Guide
            
Where should disclosures appear?

            

                
YouTubeSay the disclosure near the start and place it before sponsor or affiliate links in the description.

                
InstagramPut the disclosure before the caption truncates and use the paid partnership label when relevant.

                
TikTokUse visible caption or on-screen wording early because viewers move quickly through posts.

                
BlogPlace the disclosure before the first affiliate link, sponsored recommendation, or product CTA.

                
NewsletterDisclose before sponsored sections, affiliate links, or product recommendations inside the email.

            

        

    


    

        

            
Frequently Asked Questions

            

                
Do affiliate links require disclosure?
Yes. If you may earn a commission or receive another benefit, disclose it before users click the link or make a purchase decision.

                
Is #ad enough?
A clear #ad or #sponsored tag can help, but placement matters. It should be easy to see and not buried below "more" or hidden among many unrelated tags.

                
Does YouTube require verbal disclosure?
For sponsored, affiliate, or compensated content, a spoken disclosure near the start is a smart practice because viewers may never open the description.

                
Can I edit the generated disclosure?
Yes. Treat the generated copy as a clean starting point, then adjust the wording to match the exact brand relationship, platform, and campaign terms.

                
Is this legal advice?
No. This is a practical drafting tool for clearer creator disclosures. For legal questions about a campaign or contract, consult a qualified professional.
