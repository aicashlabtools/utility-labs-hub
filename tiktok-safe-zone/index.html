How to Use the TikTok Safe Zone Template

When creating short-form vertical video, getting text or captions covered by native interface controls can hurt clarity and retention. This free TikTok safe zone checker helps you preview graphic clearance before you post. Upload a 9:16 video frame or image into the local preview canvas to review where right-side icons, bottom captions, and top header areas may appear.

Why Does TikTok Cut Off My Text?

TikTok places mobile UI elements over every vertical video. If hooks, subtitles, logos, or calls to action sit outside the recommended TikTok text safe area dimensions, they may become harder to read. Keep key visual elements center-weighted and avoid placing important text near the right-side interaction stack or bottom caption container.
