Frequently Asked Questions

1. How accurate is the Win Probability score?

The algorithm weights pricing, authority anchoring, response urgency, and formatting gaps against standard high-ticket freelance conversion histories to output realistic data models.

2. Does a low score mean my proposal will be rejected?

No. A lower score simply highlights underlying project variables or structure issues that create friction points for your client during the evaluation process.

3. Why does Response Speed have such a heavy weight?

Data indicates that responding within the first 1 to 4 hours of active interest cycles increases project capture chances significantly compared to late responses.

4. Should I always lower my price to increase win probability?

Absolutely not. Competing solely on lowest pricing signals commoditization. Positioning yourself as a premium specialist anchors higher trust bounds.

No. The script functions entirely within your local browser's runtime environment. No remote data pipelines or cookies collect your client details.
