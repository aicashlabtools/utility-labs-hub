Frequently Asked Questions

1. How accurate is the Win Probability score?

It is an estimate, not a guarantee. The score weighs trust, urgency, positioning, response speed, and proposal quality so you can spot the biggest weak points before you follow up.

2. Does a low score mean my proposal will be rejected?

No. It means the proposal may need stronger proof, clearer scope, faster follow-up, or better positioning before the client decides.

3. Why does Response Speed have such a heavy weight?

Fast replies help while the project is still urgent in the client's mind. A thoughtful response within a few hours can keep momentum from cooling off.

4. Should I always lower my price to increase win probability?

No. Lowering price can hurt trust if it makes your work feel generic. Clear outcomes, proof, and scope often improve win probability more than a discount.

5. Are my proposal checklist details stored?

No. The calculator runs in your browser, and your checklist choices are not uploaded or stored.
