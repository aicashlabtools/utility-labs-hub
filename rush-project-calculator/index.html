Rush Project Pricing FAQ

What is a typical freelance rush fee?

A 15% to 100% premium is common as urgency rises, but the defensible amount depends on disruption, overtime, workload, and work displaced.

Should the rush fee be separate?

Yes. A separate line item keeps the base scope clear and shows that the premium pays for scheduling priority and compressed delivery.

Should rush projects be paid upfront?

For highly urgent work, a larger deposit or full upfront payment helps confirm commitment before you rearrange your schedule.

Does the calculator save my details?

No. Calculations run locally in your browser and the tool does not require an account.
