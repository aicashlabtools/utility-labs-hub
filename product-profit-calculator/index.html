Yes. This tool is free to use in your browser.

No signup is required. Open the tool, enter your details, and use the result right away.

No. The tool is designed to run locally in your browser whenever possible, so your inputs are not saved or sent to our servers.

Yes. It is designed for ebooks, courses, templates, presets, guides, Gumroad products, and digital downloads.

Yes. You can copy the profit and margin estimate into your pricing plan.

Yes. The layout is responsive, though larger screens are usually easier for detailed editing or review.
