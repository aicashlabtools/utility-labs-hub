Idea Validation Guide

How to Validate a Product Idea Before You Build

Product validation is the process of checking whether real people want a product enough to reply, join a waitlist, or pay before the full build exists. This idea validation calculator helps you separate casual interest from stronger product market fit signals.

Use the score as an early filter, then run a presell validation test when the idea shows traction. For creators, freelancers, and digital product sellers, validating a business idea before building can prevent wasted time and reveal which offer angle buyers actually care about.
