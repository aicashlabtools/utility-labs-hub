Frequently Asked Questions

            

                
What if the client will not let me share their name?
Use the anonymous permission setting and describe the client by market, size, or business type. Replace sensitive figures with approved percentages when possible.

                
Do I need perfect metrics?
No, but measurable proof makes the case study stronger. Time saved, error reduction, faster delivery, conversion lift, response rate, or reduced admin work can all be useful.

                
Where should I publish the finished case study?
Start with your portfolio or proposal template, then repurpose the same story into LinkedIn, email follow-up, and platform portfolio cards.
