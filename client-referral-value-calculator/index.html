30-Day Referral Activation Plan

Freelancer Blueprint

Identify the clients who are most likely to introduce you.

1.

List clients who recently praised your work, renewed a project, or got a clear win from the engagement.

2.

Write down two specific outcomes you helped create so the referral ask feels concrete.

3.

Look for one or two people in their network who resemble your best clients.

Best starting point: Ask clients who have already sent positive feedback in writing.

Make a simple, specific referral ask.

Email Script Template:
"Hey [Name], I loved working together on [project/result]. I have room for one or two similar clients next month. Do you know anyone who needs help with [specific problem]?"

Give people an easy way to introduce you.

->

One-page proof: Summarize the project, result, and type of client you help best.

->

Intro blurb: Write a short paragraph your client can paste into an email or DM.

Turn referrals into a repeatable habit.

Add a referral ask to your project wrap-up, milestone check-in, or monthly reporting process. The best time to ask is right after a clear win, when the client can easily remember the value you created.
