Frequently Asked Questions

How long should my freelancer profile bio be?

Most profiles should be long enough to prove credibility but easy to skim. Aim for a strong headline, a clear opening promise, two to four proof-driven paragraphs, and one CTA.

Should freelancers specialize?

Usually yes. A specific service for a specific target client makes it easier for buyers to trust your expertise and understand why your rate is higher than a generalist profile.

How often should I update my freelancer profile?

Update your freelancer profile whenever you change services, add a strong result, enter a new niche, raise your rates, or notice that profile views are not turning into qualified messages.

Can I edit the Optimized Profile Output?

Yes. Treat the output as a polished starting point, then adjust claims, tone, results, and credentials so everything is accurate and platform appropriate.
