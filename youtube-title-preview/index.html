YouTube Title Preview FAQ

What is the best length for a YouTube title?

A practical target is 45–60 characters. It provides room for a keyword and hook while reducing truncation risk.

Why do mobile and desktop previews differ?

YouTube allocates different title widths across layouts. Actual clipping also varies with letters, device width, font rendering, and experiments.

Does title length affect YouTube SEO?

Length is not a standalone ranking shortcut, but clear keywords and a visible hook can improve relevance and click-through behavior.

Should I use power words or numbers?

Use them only when they make the promise clearer. Specific numbers and one relevant emotional word can improve scanning without becoming clickbait.

How long should a YouTube Shorts title be?

YouTube Shorts titles use the same 100-character platform limit, but concise titles are easier to scan on mobile. Put the topic and hook in the first 40–50 characters whenever possible.

Are my title ideas stored?

No. This tool performs its checks locally in your browser and does not upload your title ideas.
