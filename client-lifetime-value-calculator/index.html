    Client Lifetime Value FAQ

    What is client lifetime value for a freelancer?

    Client lifetime value is the total project, retainer, upsell, and referral revenue an account is expected to generate across the working relationship.

    Should referral revenue count toward client lifetime value?

    Referral revenue can be shown separately and included in the broader relationship value when the estimate is based on real referral patterns rather than wishful assumptions.

    Why compare retaining a client with replacing one?

    Replacing revenue usually requires acquisition spend and unpaid pitching time. The comparison makes those hidden costs visible without implying that every client should be retained.

    Is a higher client lifetime value always better?

    Not by itself. Profitability, workload, concentration risk, payment behavior, and strategic fit still matter. Use lifetime revenue as one decision input.

    Does this calculator save client information?

    No. It runs locally in your browser, has no saved-client feature, and does not require an account.
