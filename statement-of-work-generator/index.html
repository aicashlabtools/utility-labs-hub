STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW)
This Statement of Work ("SOW") is issued pursuant to the agreements by and between Your Name / Agency ("Service Provider") and Client Company Name ("Client") dated June 1, 2026.
1. Project Summary & Objectives
Service Provider will deliver short-form video planning, production assets, and publishing support designed to help the client improve content output and campaign organization.
2. Scope of Work & Deliverables
The specific outcomes required under this engagement are listed below. Any deliverable not included in this list is considered outside the agreed project scope:
  • Phase 1: Review current content, goals, and competitor examples.
  • Phase 2: 5 polished short-form video production draft assets.
  • Phase 3: Final campaign plan and publishing checklist.
3. Revision Policy
Service Provider includes up to two (2) rounds of revision requests at no extra charge. Additional revisions requested after those rounds may require a separate estimate and timeline adjustment.
4. Financial Terms & Compensation Ledger
Client agrees to pay Service Provider according to the project phases and payment amounts listed below:

Important Change Provision: Any request for additional deliverables, expanded edits, new assets, or altered project requirements outside Section 2 will be treated as an Out-of-Scope Request. These requests require review, approval, and a separate estimate before work begins.
