Recommended Reward Strategy

For Business Partner connections, we recommend an balanced structure of $120 or an 4.8% commission payouts to incentivize long-term deal flow cleanly.

💡 Why this reward is safe: This keeps most of your estimated profit in the project while still giving the referrer a clear reason to send qualified leads.
📅 When to pay the reward: Disburse the reward value exclusively within 14 days following the incoming client invoice settlement clearing your account.

Alternative Reward Models

  • • Flat cash reward: $120 flat payout upon contract execution.
  • • Percentage commission: 4.8% gross contract metric tracking.
  • • Client credit: $150 credit value towards prospective milestones.
  • • Tiered referral bonus: Step commission up +2% on client's 3rd close.

Risk Warnings

  • ⚠️Do not pay before the client pays.
  • ⚠️Avoid rewards that consume too much of your profit.
  • ⚠️Put referral terms in writing.
  • ⚠️Define whether the reward is one-time or recurring.
  • ⚠️Track who referred each client cleanly.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is the Freelancer Referral Reward Calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use in your browser. No login or paid account is required.

2. What is a normal referral fee for freelancers?

Many freelancers use a flat bonus or roughly 5% to 10% of project profit, depending on the project size and relationship.

3. Should I pay a flat reward or percentage commission?

Use a flat reward for simple projects with clear pricing. Use a percentage when project values vary a lot.

4. When should I pay a referral reward?

Pay after the client has paid you and the payment has cleared. Put the timing in writing before referrals start.

5. Are my inputs uploaded?

No. Your numbers stay in your browser and are not uploaded or stored.

6. Does this replace legal or financial advice?

No. Use it as a planning tool, then confirm payment terms, taxes, and contracts with the right professional if needed.
